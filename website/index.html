How it works

From your player to the places you choose.

Aura reads what’s playing on your Mac and carries it forward — only where you’ve switched it on.

  1. Player

    Reads what you play

    Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music through YTMDesktop, and best-effort TIDAL. No plugins, no browser extension.

  2. Presence

    Shows up on Discord

    Rich Presence with reliable cover art, your choice of lines, timers, images, and a listening link.

  3. Scrobbling

    Keeps Last.fm honest

    Only qualified listens are scrobbled, and anything Last.fm can’t take right away waits and retries instead of disappearing.

Features

Quiet by design. Close at hand.

Aura runs in the background and stays out of the way until you want it.

Lives in your menu bar

Playback controls, Discord and Last.fm switches, Private Mode, and this week’s recap — one click from anywhere.

Private in one click

Pause Discord and Last.fm for fifteen minutes, an hour, or until you’re back. Your history stays on your Mac either way.

Your listening, at a glance

Local insights, top artists, busiest hours, and a weekly recap you can share — calculated on your Mac, never uploaded.

Eighteen themes

Pick one for light mode and another for dark. Aura switches with your Mac.

Nothing gets lost

When Last.fm is down, finished plays wait in Pending Plays and retry on their own. If one is rejected, fix the details and send it again.

Privacy

Local by design.

Aura runs on your Mac. You decide what leaves it, and when.

  • No account. No backend.

    Aura never asks you to create an account or connect to a server of its own.

  • Credentials stay on your Mac.

    Your Last.fm credentials and session token are kept in an owner-only local file.

  • Sharing starts when you say so.

    Discord and Last.fm stay off until you switch them on, and Private Mode is always one click away.

Version 2.0

A new name, and a new look.

PresenceFM is now Aura — rebuilt around a single luminous halo, with an iPhone companion, stronger queue recovery, and one shared vocabulary for scrobble status across both apps.

  • PresenceFM becomes Aura, with a halo mark, a warm-to-cool palette, and translucent surfaces throughout both apps.
  • Source-built iPhone companion with a searchable scrobble history and user-confirmed Apple Music history import.
  • Pending Plays gains filtering, search, and one-step retry or removal after an outage.
  • Live eligibility progress with a single, plain-language recovery action for anything that blocks a scrobble.
  • Existing listening history, credentials, and backups carry over automatically on first launch.
